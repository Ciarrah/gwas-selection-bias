Scenario

Figure 2(e). Genotype G affects the outcome Y and survival to recruitment R, and an unmeasured factor U affects both. Only people alive at recruitment are studied. Conditioning on survival induces an association between survival SNPs and U, and hence with Y.

Parents’ outcomes YP depend on the parent’s genotype GP, half of which each participant inherits, and on the parent’s own UP. They are recorded whether or not the parent survived, so they are not affected by the participant’s survival.

SNPs affect Y only (Y_only), survival only (R_only), both (both), or neither (null).

2. Diagnostic: proxy outcomes

Participants’ genotypes are regressed on their parents’ outcomes. A participant carries half of a parent’s alleles, so proxy effects are multiplied by 2.

proxy <- proxy_gwas(p$Y_P[alive], d$G[alive, ])

knitr::kable(rbind(

The proxy estimates are unbiased in every SNP group, but less precise: the proxy carries half the genetic signal, so its standard errors are about twice as large.

A SNP whose own-outcome estimate differs from its proxy estimate indicates selection on the participant’s outcome.

The difference is concentrated in SNPs that affect survival and diluted across all SNPs. Because the proxy’s standard errors dominate the comparison, this diagnostic needs large samples.

3. Correction

No correction method is retained for this structure. Where parental outcomes are available, the proxy estimates can be used in place of the biased own-outcome estimates, at the cost of precision.

4. Summary

grp <- rep(names(d$groups), lengths(d$groups))[order(unlist(d$groups))]

plot_df <- rbind(
  data.frame(analysis = "full cohort (reference)", snp_group = grp,
             truth = d$truth, beta = full$beta),
  data.frame(analysis = "survivors, own outcome", snp_group = grp,
             truth = d$truth, beta = own$beta),
  data.frame(analysis = "survivors, parental proxy", snp_group = grp,
             truth = d$truth, beta = proxy$beta)
)
plot_df$analysis <- factor(plot_df$analysis, levels = unique(plot_df$analysis))

ggplot(plot_df, aes(truth, beta, colour = snp_group)) +
  geom_abline(slope = 1, intercept = 0, linetype = "dashed", colour = "grey50") +
  geom_point(alpha = 0.7) +
  facet_wrap(~ analysis, nrow = 1) +
  labs(x = "True SNP effect", y = "Estimated SNP effect",
       colour = "SNP group") +
  theme_bw()

Points on the dashed line are unbiased. Among survivors, survival-only SNPs (true effect 0) move away from zero. Their parental proxy estimates return to the line, with more scatter.
